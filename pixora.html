Advance Connectivity

• USB Ports: The two USB ports are likely USB 3.0 (identified by the blue interior), which offer high-speed data transfer up to 5 Gbps, making them ideal for external storage, peripherals, and fast file transfers.
• HDMI Port: The HDMI port supports high-definition video and audio output, with modern versions (such as HDMI 2.1) capable of delivering 4K and even 8K resolution at higher refresh rates for smooth visuals.
• USB Type-C Port: The USB-C port is a versatile connection, often supporting fast data transfer, charging, and even video output (depending on specifications like Thunderbolt 4 or USB4), making it a powerful all-in-one solution.
